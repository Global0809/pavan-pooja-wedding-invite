# Pavan & Pooja invitation

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Wedding guests opening a shared public invitation, especially on mobile phones.

## Product Purpose

Invite guests to Pavan and Pooja's celebrations on 20–21 November 2026 at Fairfield by Marriott, Gogte Plaza, Belagavi - 591113. Make the programme, location and interactive wedding world easy to access.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages. Preserve the configured dates and times, two supplied films, scroll-driven frame sequences, countdown, audio controls and separate 3D portal route. No scratch card or RSVP. Keep surnames omitted. Prioritize light assets, smooth mobile scrolling, touch controls and reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

User requests an entirely new theme, layout and UI and explicitly selects a darker evening theme. The current redesign supersedes the prior burgundy/gold and Comic Sans glass styling on the invitation.

## Evidence on Hand

config.js is the schedule and venue authority. Existing assets include film1.mp4, film2.mp4, origami frame sequences, stills and audio. The world lives in world/index.html.
